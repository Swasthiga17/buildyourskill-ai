# BuildYourSkill AI — Private Beta Tester Guide

Welcome to the **BuildYourSkill AI Private Beta**! You are among the first cohort of engineers and learners testing our next-generation adaptive AI learning platform.

---

## 1. What is BuildYourSkill AI?

BuildYourSkill AI creates a living **AI Knowledge Twin** of your technical capabilities. Rather than static video courses, the platform uses real Google Gemini LLM intelligence, RAG vector retrieval, and AST-isolated sandbox verification to provide:
- **Dynamic Skill Gap Engine:** Detects your exact technical deficiencies relative to Staff AI/ML Engineer expectations.
- **Context-Grounded AI Mentor:** Solves architecture questions, explains PyTorch autograd DAGs, and generates Mermaid system diagrams.
- **Mock Interview Evaluator:** Conducts real-time interview evaluations with depth and accuracy rubric scoring.
- **Project Blueprint Generator:** Designs multi-phase production machine learning projects.

---

## 2. How to Join & Register

1. Open the platform URL: [http://localhost:5173](http://localhost:5173) (or your deployment URL).
2. Click **Get Started / Sign In** and switch to **Create Account**.
3. Enter your Name, Email, Password, and your **Private Beta Invite Code** (e.g. `BS-AI-7KQ9-X2M4`).
4. Click **Create Beta Account**.

> [!NOTE]
> Invite codes are single-use or limited-use cryptographic tokens configured for the `aug-2026` beta cohort.

---

## 3. Your Guided 4-Step Onboarding

When you first log in, you will see the **Private Beta Welcome Banner** with a 4-step checklist:

1. **Step 1: Complete Initial Skill Assessment**
   - Head to `/assessments` and complete a 3-minute diagnostic test in Python, Machine Learning, or PyTorch.
2. **Step 2: Inspect Your Knowledge Twin & Skill Gaps**
   - Navigate to `/knowledge-twin` to see your Bayesian confidence scores, domain radars, and prioritized skill gaps.
3. **Step 3: Ask the AI Mentor for Architecture Advice**
   - Go to `/mentor` and ask a technical question (e.g., *"How do PyTorch forward/backward hooks optimize memory in distributed training?"*).
4. **Step 4: Launch Today's Plan**
   - Open `/today-plan` to review your personalized daily curriculum.

---

## 4. How to Submit Feedback & Report Inaccuracies

Throughout your beta testing, you have access to two feedback mechanisms:

1. **Floating "Beta Feedback" Button:**
   - Click the green **Beta Feedback** badge located in the bottom-right corner of any page.
   - Select the feature area (AI Mentor, Mock Interview, Project Generator, etc.).
   - Rate your experience (1 to 5 stars) and write your comments.
2. **In-Context AI Rating:**
   - After receiving an AI Mentor explanation, interview evaluation, or project blueprint, submit immediate feedback.

---

## 5. What We Expect from Beta Testers

- **AI Reasoning Accuracy:** Did the AI Mentor's explanation make technical sense? Were the mock interview rubric scores fair?
- **Workflow Coherence:** Did your Knowledge Twin update after you completed an assessment or interview?
- **Speed & Usability:** Did pages load promptly? Was the code execution sandbox responsive?
